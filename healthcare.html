Compliance posture

HIPAA-aware. BAA-eligible. Operationalized — not just claimed.

HIPAA compliance is a deployment posture, not a checkbox. Here is how we operationalize it for every healthcare engagement:

  • BAAs with subprocessors. Voice (Twilio under BAA), LLM (OpenAI Enterprise or Anthropic with applicable BAA), transcription, recording storage — all on BAA-eligible plans before a single PHI call is taken.
  • Mutual BAA with your practice. Signed before deployment. Subprocessor list disclosed and updated.
  • Minimum necessary by design. The agent only collects the PHI the workflow requires. Voice recordings retained 90 days by default, configurable to your policy.
  • Encryption + access logs. At rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+). Access to recordings and transcripts is logged with user, timestamp, and reason.
  • Escalation by clinical protocol. Red-flag symptoms (chest pain, SI, stroke signs) route to 911 prompt + on-call clinician per your written protocol — not the AI's judgment.
  • Disclosure in the greeting. Patients are told they're speaking to an automated assistant, with an immediate path to a human.

We are not a HIPAA-certifying body and we do not warrant compliance for your practice. We deploy against the rules and provide documentation suitable for your security officer's review.

FAQ

Healthcare questions, answered honestly.

Is this HIPAA compliant?

HIPAA compliance is a posture, not a checkbox — and it lives at the deployment, not the technology. We deploy on BAA-eligible voice and LLM vendors and sign a BAA with your practice. PHI handling is configured per workflow — minimum necessary, logged access, encryption at rest and in transit. We are not a HIPAA-certifying body; we operationalize against the rules.

Which EHRs do you integrate with?

Athena, eClinicalWorks, Practice Fusion, Kareo/Tebra, DrChrono, NextGen, Greenway, Allscripts, and Epic (via App Orchard or webhook bridge). If your EHR has an API or HL7/FHIR endpoint, we can integrate.

Can the agent triage clinical complaints?

Triage is configurable but conservative by design. The agent identifies red-flag symptoms from your protocol list and escalates immediately. We do not let the agent give clinical advice.

Behavioral / mental health calls?

We script for 988 crisis-line escalation, warm clinician handoff, and screening (PHQ-9, GAD-7) where the practice uses them. The agent never attempts counseling.

Will patients know they're talking to AI?

Yes — disclosure is in the greeting by default. Several states require AI disclosure; we configure to the strictest applicable rule.

Multilingual?

English and Spanish included. Mandarin, Vietnamese, Arabic, French, Russian and others have been deployed. Interpreter-line warm transfer is also supported.

What about state laws like CMIA (California) or telephone-recording consent?

We configure the deployment to the strictest applicable rule across the states you operate in. Two-party consent states get explicit recording disclosure; one-party states get our standard disclosure. CMIA and similar state laws are reviewed during scoping.
