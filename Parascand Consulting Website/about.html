About Parascand Consulting

AI that ships — live in the tools your business already uses.

Parascand Consulting helps service businesses across Virginia stop losing leads. We build AI front desk agents, voice and chat bots, and automations that answer every call and message, qualify prospects, and book appointments — around the clock.

We're not your typical agency

We don't just hand you software and walk away. We work with you to map how leads reach your business, find where they're slipping through the cracks, and deploy AI systems that plug the gaps — then we stay involved to make sure the growth continues.

Founded by James Parascand, Parascand Consulting focuses on real, shipped results: AI voice agents and chatbots that answer every quote request and call, missed-call text-back that catches the rush, and automated follow-up that nurtures leads into signed clients.

Why businesses choose us

  • Everything in one platform — phone, text, email, chat and social in a single inbox
  • AI that answers 24/7 so you never miss a lead again
  • Automation that follows up, reminds, and books without manual work
  • Local focus across Northern Virginia, Fredericksburg and Richmond
  • Fast setup — most systems live within days, not months

Who we serve

From accounting firms and insurance agencies to restaurants, home services, medical practices and more — if your business runs on calls, leads and booked appointments, we can help you capture more of them.
