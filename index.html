Questions we hear weekly

Direct answers.

How much does an AI voice agent cost for a small business?
Less than you'd expect — usually a fraction of one part-time hire. Plans are sized for small-business budgets, starting with an affordable self-service option. You also get a free website evaluation up front, and a free website build when you start a plan. Because the right setup depends on your call volume and tools, we quote each business after a quick, free fit call instead of publishing a one-size-fits-all rate. Call (571) 332-3829 for your free quote. How pricing works →
Can AI really take phone orders for my restaurant, end-to-end?
Yes. Our restaurant phone-AI integrates directly with Toast, Square, Clover, Olo, and Aloha. Orders ring into your kitchen ticket printer or KDS exactly as if a host took them. Most single-location restaurants we onboard recover $3,000–$5,000/month in previously missed orders — payback under 30 days.
What's the difference between you and Slang.ai or Kea.ai?
They sell their platform. We're vendor-neutral — we pick the right stack for your restaurant and integrate accordingly. We resell or rebuild on GHL Voice AI + POS, depending on your scale. If Slang or Kea is right for you, we'll tell you and walk you to their door. We get paid for the answer, not for the platform.
Do you do AI for any industry, or only the verticals listed?
We're vertical-flexible — we work with any service-based business. Restaurants, dental, healthcare, real estate, and home services are concentrations where we have repeatable playbooks, so onboarding is faster and ROI more predictable. Other industries are welcome with a standard AI readiness audit up front.
How fast can you ship?
Front Desk Assistant: 2–3 weeks from signed proposal to live. Vertical overlays (restaurant POS, dental PMS, healthcare EHR): add 1–2 weeks. Workflow automation projects: 1–6 weeks depending on scope. Custom agents: 4–10 weeks. AI readiness audit: 5 business days. We don't promise overnight — we promise weekly demos so you see what you're buying as we build it.
What's a fractional CAIO?
Chief AI Officer on retainer. We sit in your leadership cadence one day a week (or biweekly), pressure-test AI decisions, prioritize the roadmap, run vendor evaluations, and own the change management. Custom-quoted per engagement (3-month minimum).
Are you HIPAA-compliant for healthcare clients?
We deploy HIPAA-aligned stacks (Anthropic enterprise tier with BAA, AWS Bedrock, GHL Pro with BAA) when PHI is involved. Many AI use cases in medical practices can be scoped to non-PHI flows — that's the first question in the readiness audit. We don't oversell compliance, and we don't accept HIPAA engagements we can't honor.
Where are you located?
Virginia-based, serving the whole DMV — Northern Virginia (Arlington, Alexandria, Fairfax, Loudoun, Prince William), Washington DC, and Maryland (Bethesda, Silver Spring, Rockville, Montgomery & Prince George's County) — plus Richmond and Charlottesville.
