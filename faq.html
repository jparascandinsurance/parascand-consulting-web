Pricing & packages

Cost, contracts, and what's included.

How much do your packages cost?

Our plans are built for small-business budgets — usually a fraction of one part-time hire — with an affordable self-service option. Every business gets a free website evaluation, and a free website build when you start a plan. After a free fit call we send you a fixed, written quote tailored to your shop — no surprises. The core bundle is voice AI (inbound + outbound) + website chatbot + SMS + email + a managed GoHighLevel CRM + pre-built workflows, with tailored add-ons for restaurants, dental, and healthcare. Simple, honest pricing — quoted free on a fit call.

What does "unlimited usage" actually cover?

Unlimited within reasonable business use. A typical SMB — single location, normal call/message volume — will never hit a limit. We explicitly call this out: "Excessive automated traffic or commercial reselling subject to review." If you're a high-volume call center, you're an enterprise customer and we'll quote you a custom plan.

Is ad spend included in the Growth plan?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta/Google. We manage the campaigns, optimize them, and report — but the media budget is yours. We add a 10% management fee on combined ad spend above $2,000/month; at SMB-scale budgets this rarely triggers. Google LSA typically runs $10–$15 per qualified lead for service businesses. Facebook Ads is pay-per-click; performance varies significantly by vertical and creative.

What's a vertical overlay?

For restaurants, dental, and healthcare, we add specialized integrations on top of the Front Desk Assistant base. Restaurant: POS integration + bilingual EN/ES + menu ingestion (Toast/Square/Clover/Olo/Aloha). Dental: PMS integration, HIPAA-aware, new-patient intake, recall, insurance verification, emergency triage (OpenDental/Dentrix/Eaglesoft/iDentalSoft/Curve). Healthcare: EHR-integrated, BAA-backed, HIPAA-aligned deployment supporting behavioral health intake. Real estate runs on the standard Front Desk base — no overlay needed. Add-ons are quoted on your free call. Custom quote per vertical.

What's your cancellation policy?

The Front Desk packages are month-to-month after a 90-day commit (we need that long to tune). Workflow automation projects are fixed-price, fixed-scope — you pay for the build, then own it. Fractional CAIO is 3-month minimum, monthly thereafter.

Do you mark up platform fees (Zapier, OpenAI, etc.)?

No. Pass-through at cost. Every proposal discloses the expected platform costs for the workflow we're building — Zapier task counts, Make ops, OpenAI tokens, voice minute charges. You pay those vendors directly where possible, or we reimburse-bill at cost.

Scope & engagement

What we build and what we won't.

Will you tell me AI isn't the right answer?

Yes — and we do, regularly. About 1 in 5 fit calls and audits ends with us recommending against an AI engagement, usually because the underlying process is broken or because the volume doesn't justify the build. We'd rather lose the project than ship something that fails in six months.

What won't you build?

Three categories: (1) Outbound voice campaigns to cold-purchased lists. (2) Replacement of licensed-professional judgment — agents giving medical, legal, or financial advice. (3) AI that can move money, send irreversible communications, or modify production systems without a human-in-the-loop confirmation step in front of the irreversible action.

Do you have references?

Yes, on request, after a fit call. We don't publish a client list — most SMB clients prefer privacy.

How quickly can you start?

Front Desk Assistant: 2–3 weeks from signed proposal to live. Workflow automation: 1–6 weeks depending on scope. Custom agents: 4–10 weeks. Fit call to signed proposal usually inside one week.

Do you take engagements outside Virginia?

Yes. We're based in Virginia, Washington DC & Maryland (the DMV) but remote-first. Most engagements are remote. We travel for fractional CAIO clients and for large initial deployments — travel billed at cost.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes — mutual NDAs as standard. For healthcare engagements we sign BAAs. Contracts are short and in plain English. We work off a one-page master service agreement and per-engagement statements of work.

Technology & security

What we use. How we secure it.

What platforms do you build on?

Voice AI: GoHighLevel, Vapi, Retell, ElevenLabs depending on use case. LLMs: Anthropic Claude (default), OpenAI when the use case demands it. Workflow: Zapier, Make.com, n8n, Pipedream, custom Node/Python. Hosting: Railway, Fly.io, your own infrastructure where preferred.

How is my data handled?

Encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+). We don't train on your data. We sign mutual NDAs and, for healthcare, BAAs. Access logs are maintained on PHI-bearing systems. We use the strictest applicable retention policy — 90 days default on call recordings, configurable to your policy.

Are you HIPAA-compliant?

HIPAA compliance is a posture, not a checkbox — and it lives at the deployment. For healthcare engagements, we deploy on BAA-eligible voice and LLM vendors (Twilio under BAA, OpenAI Enterprise with BAA, Anthropic Enterprise where applicable), sign a BAA with your practice, configure minimum-necessary PHI handling, and provide documentation suitable for your security officer's review.

Do you support SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / state privacy laws?

We're not SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certified as a firm (we're a one-principal practice — those certifications are scoped to org-of-record systems we don't operate). For enterprise engagements requiring formal certifications, we partner with certified subprocessors and document the data flow for your security review. We comply with applicable state privacy laws (CCPA, CMIA, VCDPA, etc.) on the deployments we build.

What happens if I want to leave?

You own the data, the configurations, the call recordings, the CRM records. We hand off documentation, runbooks, and access. No lock-in beyond the natural switching cost of "you'd need to rebuild this somewhere." We give you the keys.
