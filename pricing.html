About "unlimited usage": Unlimited within reasonable business use. Excessive automated traffic or commercial reselling is subject to review. A normal SMB will never hit a limit.

About ad spend on the $1,497 Growth package: Ad spend is not included — that's paid directly to Meta and Google. We manage the campaigns, optimize them, and report. The media budget is yours. Above $2,000/month in combined ad spend we add a 10% management fee on the overage — at SMB-scale budgets this rarely triggers.

Google Local Services Ads (LSA): We typically see $10–$15 per lead for service businesses. This is the recommended channel for plumbers, HVAC, dental, real estate, and similar — the lead arrives prequalified and Google guarantees it.

Facebook Ads: Pay-per-click model, not pay-per-lead. Performance varies significantly by vertical and creative. Generally lower efficiency than LSA for service businesses, but useful for awareness, lookalike targeting, and retargeting.

What you don't pay for

Three things we don't bill.

1. The fit call. 20–30 minutes. No deck. We'll tell you whether the $497 package is right, whether you need an upgrade, or whether AI isn't the right answer at all.

2. Markups on ad spend or platform fees. Meta and Google charge you directly for ad budgets. Zapier, Make, n8n, OpenAI token costs — pass-through at cost, disclosed in the proposal.

3. Annual lock-in. 90-day commit on monthly packages (we need that long to tune). After that, cancel any time. Project work is fixed-price — you pay for the build and own it.
