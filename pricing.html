Voice agent

Answers every call, 24/7. Books appointments. Qualifies leads. Routes urgent calls to you. Bilingual EN/ES. Transcripts logged.

C

Website chatbot

Trained on your business, your services, your FAQ. Captures leads, books calls, escalates to a human when it should.

Your full GHL workspace, fully set up. CRM + contact management, sales pipeline tracking, calendar/booking, automated reviews, marketing automation, funnel/form builder, reporting, mobile app for the field.

Re-engagement campaigns, follow-up sequences, review-request automations, appointment reminders, win-back nudges — customized to your vertical, not generic templates.

Light content updates, 1–2 business days. Hours, menu changes, new service pages, copy edits, image swaps. Major redesigns quoted separately.

What you don't pay for

Four things we don't bill.

1. The fit call. 30 minutes. No deck. We'll tell you which plan is right, whether you need an upgrade, or whether AI isn't the right answer at all.

2. The website evaluation. A full review of your current site — free, whether or not you ever hire us. Start a plan and the new website build is free too.

3. Full retail on platform fees. Meta and Google bill your ad budget directly. For tools like Zapier, Make, n8n, and AI usage, our partner and volume discounts get passed on to you — every proposal discloses the expected platform costs up front.

4. Annual lock-in. A short commit on monthly plans (we need that long to tune). After that, cancel any time. Project work is fixed-price — you pay for the build and own it.
