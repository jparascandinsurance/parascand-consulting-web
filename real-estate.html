Sample call — Zillow lead qualification

What the agent does with a 10:42 PM lead.

Thursday 10:42 PM · Outbound from new Zillow lead · Follow Up Boss

Agent (Olivia): Hi, is this Marcus? This is Olivia calling from the Henderson Team — you just inquired about 412 Oak Ridge Road on Zillow. Is now a bad time?

Caller: Uh, no — go ahead.

Olivia: Great. Just a few quick questions so we can get you the right help. Are you looking to buy in the next 90 days, or just early in the process?

Caller: Probably the next two or three months. We're moving for my wife's job.

Olivia: Got it — relocating, so timeline's real. Have you been pre-approved by a lender yet, or do you need a recommendation?

Caller: Pre-approved through Rocket. $525,000.

Olivia: Perfect. And are you looking at 412 Oak Ridge specifically, or is that one of several you're considering?

Caller: Several. We've got maybe ten on our list, but Oak Ridge is one of the top ones.

Olivia: Excellent. I'd like to get you on the calendar with one of our buyer specialists this week — they'll pull all ten properties, do an MLS walkthrough with you, and schedule showings for the ones that hold up. We have openings Friday at 5 or Saturday morning at 10. Which works?

Caller: Saturday at 10 is good.

Olivia: Done. You'll be working with Marisa Lopez — I'm texting you her details and the meeting link now. She'll call you Saturday at 10. Anything else I can answer tonight?

Caller: Nope — appreciate the quick callback.

Olivia: Of course. Have a good night, Marcus.

Result: Lead created in Follow Up Boss at 10:44 PM, tagged "Qualified · Relocating · Pre-approved $525K · 90 day." Showing-prep meeting booked on Marisa's calendar. SMS sent. Call duration 2m 51s.

Real estate FAQ

Questions from team leads and brokers.

Which CRMs do you integrate with?
Native API: Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, BoomTown, Sierra Interactive, HubSpot, Wise Agent, LionDesk. Zapier/Make bridge: anything else with an API. Lead source integrations: Zillow Premier Agent, Realtor.com, Homes.com, BoldLeads, Facebook Lead Ads.
How does the AI handle showing requests for occupied listings?
The AI checks the listing's showing instructions in the MLS or your CRM. For occupied listings requiring seller approval, it offers two times and tells the buyer "I'll confirm in the next hour" — then triggers a workflow to your listing agent for approval. Confirmed time is texted back to the buyer.
What about real estate compliance — does the AI follow up like a licensed agent?
The AI is a triage and scheduling tool, not a licensed agent. It does not make representations about property condition, market value, or fiduciary advice. It identifies itself as an assistant, captures the lead, and books with a licensed agent. Compliance language is configurable per state.
Can the AI run open-house follow-up?
Yes. We integrate with whatever open-house signup tool you use (Zurple, kvCORE forms, paper-and-pen scanned), and the AI calls each visitor within 24 hours to qualify and offer a buyer consult. Increases consult-rate from 8–12% to 25–35% in our deployments.
Will my agents resist this?
The good ones won't — the AI hands them only the qualified leads. The team members who lose out are the ones who claimed leads and never worked them. We position it as elevation, not replacement: "you spend less time on tire-kickers, more time on closes." That conversation is part of the build.
What does it cost compared to virtual ISA services?
Conversion Monster, Real Geeks ISA, Smart Inside Sales run $1,500–$3,500/month for a human ISA team. Our Front Desk Assistant comes in well below those ISA shops — usually a fraction of one part-time hire, with a Team tier that adds ad management, quoted free on a fit call — except it answers in 60 seconds, works 24/7, and never has turnover. The honest answer is: a great human ISA still beats AI on rapport. AI wins on speed, volume, and consistency. Many teams run both.
