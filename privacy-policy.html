Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 24, 2026. Last updated: May 24, 2026.

Parascand Consulting LLC ("Parascand Consulting," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have. By using our website at parascandconsulting.business or engaging us as a client, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Who we are

Parascand Consulting LLC is a Virginia-based AI consulting firm serving small and mid-size businesses across the United States. Our principal place of business is in the Commonwealth of Virginia. You can reach us at parascandconsulting@gmail.com or (571) 332-3829.

2. Information we collect

We collect personal information you provide directly when you:

  • Submit a contact request through our website chat widget
  • Email, call, or text us
  • Book a fit call through our calendar embed
  • Engage us as a client and provide information needed to deliver services

Categories of personal information we may collect include: name, email address, phone number, business name, business address, industry, the message content you send us, and any additional information you voluntarily share.

We also collect limited technical information automatically when you visit our website, including IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and time on page. This information is collected through standard server logs and is used for security and analytics only.

3. SMS messaging consent and phone number collection

We do not share or sell SMS opt-in consent or phone numbers with any third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Information collected through SMS opt-in, including your phone number and consent record, will not be shared, sold, rented, or otherwise transferred to third parties for their own marketing purposes under any circumstance.

When you provide your phone number through our website chat widget, by phone call, or by direct text message, you may opt in to receive SMS messages from Parascand Consulting. These messages may include:

  • Replies to questions you submitted
  • Calendar links and appointment confirmations for fit calls
  • Follow-up communications related to your inquiry or engagement
  • Service notifications for active clients

Message frequency varies based on your interaction with us — typically no more than 10 messages per month for a given recipient.

Message and data rates may apply depending on your wireless plan. Parascand Consulting is not responsible for charges imposed by your carrier.

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message we send. You will receive a one-time confirmation that you have been unsubscribed. To request assistance, reply HELP to any message — we will respond with our contact information.

If you opt out, we may still contact you by email or phone where you have provided that consent separately.

4. How we use your information

We use the personal information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide requested information
  • Schedule and conduct fit calls and client meetings
  • Deliver consulting services to active clients
  • Send transactional and relationship messages (appointment confirmations, follow-ups, service-related communications)
  • Send infrequent marketing or newsletter communications, but only where you have provided affirmative consent
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations
  • Protect our rights and the rights of others

5. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share personal information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers we use to operate our business — including GoHighLevel (our CRM and chat-widget provider), Stripe or comparable payment processors for invoicing, and email infrastructure providers. These vendors are bound by their own privacy policies and security obligations and are permitted to use your information only to perform services for us.
  • Active client engagements — if you are an active client, we may share relevant information with vendors we deploy on your behalf (e.g., POS integration partners, voice-AI platform vendors). We will discuss any such sharing with you in advance.
  • Legal obligations — when required by law, court order, regulation, or to respond to a valid legal request.
  • Business transfers — in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of business assets, in which case the acquiring entity will be required to honor the commitments of this Privacy Policy.

We never share SMS opt-in consent or phone numbers with third parties for their marketing purposes. This restriction applies regardless of any other sharing described above.

6. How we protect your information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information against loss, theft, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. These include encrypted data transmission, access controls limiting who at the firm can see your data, and standard security practices appropriate to a small business.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

8. Your rights

Depending on your state of residence, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of certain uses of your information, including marketing
  • Withdraw consent at any time (including SMS opt-in, by replying STOP)

To exercise any of these rights, email us at parascandconsulting@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and as required by applicable law.

9. Children's privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 18, we will delete it.

10. Cookies and tracking

Our website uses minimal cookies, primarily for the functioning of the chat widget and for standard server-side analytics. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. Your browser can be configured to refuse cookies — doing so may limit certain features of the site, such as the chat widget.

11. Third-party links

Our website may link to third-party websites (e.g., LinkedIn). This Privacy Policy does not apply to those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
