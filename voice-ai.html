How the math works

Per-minute economics, explained.

Every voice agent vendor charges per minute. Most agencies won't tell you the underlying numbers. We will.

What a minute costs us (the platform layer): $0.07–$0.31 depending on stack. GoHighLevel runs $0.13/min all-in for voice + STT + TTS. Retell base is $0.07/min plus LLM. Vapi runs $0.13–$0.31. ElevenLabs voice alone is ~$0.04/min.

What that minute is worth to your business: A receptionist costs $25/hr loaded. One hour of receptionist time = 60 minutes. We charge $0.50/min overage on the Growth tier. You're paying $30 worth of AI to displace $25 worth of human time — but the AI works 24/7, answers every call, and never has a bad day.

That's why our included-minute buckets are generous. Most clients never hit them. Overage is structured at a margin, not a gotcha.
