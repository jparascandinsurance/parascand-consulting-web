How we work

Five rules we don't break.

1

Tell the truth, even when it costs the engagement.

About 1 in 5 audits ends with us telling the client to fix process first. We'd rather lose the project than ship a brittle automation that fails in six months.

2

One principal on every engagement.

The person you talk to on the fit call is the person who scopes it, builds it, and answers when something breaks. No bait-and-switch to a junior.

3

Fixed price, written scope.

Proposals are fixed-price with explicit "out of scope" lists. Platform pass-through costs are disclosed. No time-and-materials surprises.

4

You can fire us.

Documentation, Loom walkthrough, runbook. You should be able to keep the automation running without us. We don't build lock-in by hiding the keys.

5

The right tool, not the favorite tool.

We're not a Zapier shop. We're not an n8n shop. We pick the platform after mapping the workflow. About 60% lands on Make.com — but that's a result, not a starting point.

6

No outbound to cold-purchased lists.

We won't deploy outbound voice AI to cold purchased data. We will deploy outbound to your sphere, your past clients, or your opted-in pipeline — DNC-scrubbed.
